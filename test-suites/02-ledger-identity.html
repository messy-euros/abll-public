Ledger Identity — alias resolution

One person, many keys: a Zeffy contact UUID (from chip payments) and a normalised email (from cash top-ups) must resolve to a single account. New aliases attach automatically; when two existing accounts turn out to be the same person, they merge instead of splitting the balance.

Run the suite against:

Contract

resolve({contactId?, email?, label?}) -> {accountId, merged}
    - finds the account by ANY known alias (contact id or normalised email)
    - a new alias on a known account is attached (future lookups by it work)
    - if two different accounts are matched, they MERGE (merged:true); balance combines
topup({contactId?, email?}, cents, source, ref) -> credits the resolved account; idempotent on ref
debit(accountId, cents)                          -> a bet; hits the resolved (merged) account
balanceForIdentity({contactId?, email?})         -> balance via any alias
accounts()                                        -> distinct accounts (post-merge)
recover(log)                                       -> identity graph + balances rebuilt from the log

Money and identity are both events in one append-only log:
  open_account · attach_alias · merge · topup · debit · payout
